Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need a new WhatsApp number?
No. In most cases you can connect the WhatsApp Business number you already use. If you don't have one yet, we help you register it during setup.
What does "Web App" mean here?
It's the same assistant embedded as a chat widget on your own restaurant website — not a separate app to download. You add one snippet, and it shares the same menu and orders as WhatsApp.
How long does setup take?
Most restaurants are verified and live within about ten minutes through the Build Your Assistant wizard.
Can I edit the menu myself?
Yes — the Menu page in the dashboard lets you add dishes, sizes and prices without touching any code.
Is there a setup fee?
Menu import and WhatsApp connection are included free with every plan.
