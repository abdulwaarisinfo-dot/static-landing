02 Reservations

Table bookings, taken the same way as orders

A separate flow from ordering — customers ask for a table, the bot checks availability for the date and party size, and it lands on your Reservations board, not mixed in with food orders.

  • 1 Customer messages "Book a table for 4, Saturday 8 PM" — on WhatsApp or your website widget.
  • 2 The bot checks table availability for that date and time against your Reservations calendar.
  • 3 A confirmation is sent instantly, with a reminder before the booking.
  • 4 Your staff sees it on a dedicated Reservations board — separate from the Live Orders board.

07 Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need a new WhatsApp number?
No. In most cases you can connect the WhatsApp Business number you already use. If you don't have one yet, we help you register it during setup.
What does "Web App" mean here?
It's the same assistant embedded as a chat widget on your own restaurant website — not a separate app to download. You add one snippet, and it shares the same menu and orders as WhatsApp.
How long does setup take?
Most restaurants are verified and live within about ten minutes through the Build Your Assistant wizard.
Can I edit the menu myself?
Yes — the Menu page in the dashboard lets you add dishes, sizes and prices without touching any code.
Are table reservations mixed in with food orders?
No — reservations have their own board and calendar in the dashboard, kept separate from the Live Orders board so your front-of-house and kitchen workflows don't overlap.
Is there a setup fee?
Menu import and WhatsApp connection are included free with every plan.
