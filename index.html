About FORME

FORME is a fictional brand created to test an online shopping experience. Its idea is simple: a small set of everyday pieces that work together.

There is no company history, factory or certification behind it. The product details are sample data for prototyping.

Size guide

Demo example measurements for a regular-fit tee, not verified garment measurements.

How to measure: for chest, wrap a tape around the fullest part of your chest, keeping it level. For length, measure from the base of the neck to where you want the hem to fall. All values are in centimetres (cm).
