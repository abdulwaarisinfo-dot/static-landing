Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need a new WhatsApp number?
No. In most cases you can connect the WhatsApp Business number your customers already message. If you don't have a WhatsApp Business number yet, we'll help you register one during setup.
How long does connecting my number take?
Most restaurants are verified and live within about ten minutes — you enter your number, confirm the SMS code, and the bot starts answering.
Do my customers need to install anything?
No. They message your existing number like they would a friend, or open your web app link — no app to download and no account to create.
Can I edit the menu myself?
Yes — the Menu page in the dashboard lets you add dishes, sizes, spice levels and extras without touching any code. Both the bot and the web app update instantly.
What happens if the bot can't answer something?
The conversation can be handed to a human teammate at any point, right inside the same WhatsApp thread the customer is already in.
Is there a setup fee?
Menu import and WhatsApp number connection are included free with every plan — reach out and our team gets you live within a few days if you'd like a hand.
