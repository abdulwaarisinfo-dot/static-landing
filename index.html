Questions

Frequently asked

Do my customers need to install anything?
No. They message your existing WhatsApp Business number like they would a friend. The web chat widget and web app options also need no install.
How do orders reach my kitchen?
Every order placed through the bot appears immediately on the Live Orders board inside the CRM, with a blinking highlight so nothing gets missed.
Can I edit the menu myself?
Yes — the Menu page in the CRM lets you add dishes, sizes, spice levels and extras without touching any code.
Which languages does the bot speak?
English, Urdu and German out of the box, with FAQs and greeting suggestions editable per language from the CRM.
Is there a setup fee?
Menu import and WhatsApp Business connection are included free with every plan — reach out and our team gets you live within a few days.
